### India Launches AI Governance and Economic Group (AIGEG)
**Source:** AI News Digest

India's government constituted the AI Governance and Economic Group (AIGEG), a high-level inter-ministerial body chaired by Union IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw. Unlike typical AI committees, AIGEG carries an explicit labour-market mandate: assess which job profiles AI will hit first, map geographic concentration, and develop mitigation and transition plans that account for informality, skill diversity, and regional variation.
